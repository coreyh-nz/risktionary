# Post-game survey questions

- **Given:** online, after play, run by the session facilitator.
- **Anonymous:** responses have no participant ID.
- **Scale:** agreement statements use a seven-point scale: strongly disagree, disagree, somewhat disagree, neither agree nor disagree, somewhat agree, agree, strongly agree.
- **Grouping:** statements under the same number appeared under one heading.

> **Before reusing this survey:** the pilot could not link survey answers to the feedback condition each player was assigned (see the report's Discussion). For the full study, add a participant identifier that is matched to the game data. Also keep each player's condition fixed for the whole session, and consider adding a pre/post test of risk comprehension.
>
> **Data handling:** the raw Qualtrics export includes IP address and location columns. Delete them before sharing the data.
